HOA Dues

Currently, $250 per month.

  • Payment is due on the 1st of each month.
  • Payment is late if not received by the 10th of the month.

Lawn Mowing & Landscape Maintenance

Landscaping & Lawn Care are contracted by Silver Creek HOA and scheduled to service our community two days per week — Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

  • Weather permitting, all lawns (front, side, and back) will be mowed and edged during the growing season (February – October).
  • Fertilizer and Weed Control will be applied twice a year to all lawns (front and back).
  • All trees and shrubs (up to 10' tall) will be pruned in the Spring and Fall (twice per year) in both front and back yards.
Water Timer Adjustment

The timer should only be adjusted by the contracted Landscaper and/or the Board Member over Landscaping.

Use the Homeowner Portal to notify the HOA of any watering problems.

What Insurance Do I Need?

Maintaining sufficient insurance is the responsibility of the homeowner. Each homeowner should purchase a personal home insurance policy.

Contact your provider and let them know the HOA provides home structural coverage with a $25,000 deductible required. Your personal policy should cover that deductible, all personal belongings/furnishings, and Personal Liability.

Proof of Insurance for Myself or Mortgage Holder

Silver Creek Townhomes Association (HOA) insurance docs are available on the Insurance page:

  • Insurance Summary (Homeowner Letter of Coverage)
  • Certificate of Liability Insurance
  • Certificate of Property Insurance

Ask your personal insurance provider for a certificate of coverage for your personal policy.

Special Assessments

If regular monthly dues do not cover operating expenses or if funds are needed for a special project, the Board may approve a Special Assessment in addition to regular monthly dues.

Reserve Fund

The Reserve Fund accumulates money from regular monthly dues to cover expected future costs of repairing, replacing, or restoring common areas (e.g., roads, clubhouse, pools). The amount set aside is evaluated annually.

Reinvestment Fees

Silver Creek HOA does NOT charge a Reinvestment Fee at closing.

How Many Homes Are in Silver Creek?

There are 162 homes. All units feature secluded backyards.

Is Silver Creek a Senior Community?

No. Silver Creek is a community of individual family homes with residents of all ages.

Are Rentals Allowed?

No. All leases, rentals, or subletting of any kind are strictly prohibited. Units may not be used for Airbnb, VRBO, or short-term host rentals.

RV & Commercial Vehicle Parking

All streets in Silver Creek are private streets. Parking of Recreational Vehicles or commercial vehicles on streets or parking spaces is not allowed (pickup-size work trucks are permitted).

Temporary parking (<8 hours) by service providers or movers is allowed.

Can I Have Pets?

Yes. Up to two household pets are welcome per home.

Yard Sales

Yard/Garage/Estate Sales are permitted only under special circumstances and require an Exception Request approved by the HOA Board prior to the sale.

Trash Pick Up & Schedule

Trash is collected every Wednesday as early as 7:00 am. BluCan (Recycling) is collected every other Wednesday.

Contacts:

  • Washington County Solid Waste (WCSW): (435) 673-2813
  • Republic Services: (435) 628-2821 (Mon–Fri 7:30am–5pm)
BluCan / Recycle Bins Rules

Acceptable Items:

  • Plastic bottles & food containers (rinsed, no Styrofoam)
  • Paper, envelopes, junk mail, newspapers, magazines
  • Cardboard, posterboard, milk cartons, frozen food boxes
  • Aluminum/steel cans & scrap metal
  • Shredded paper must be in a clear bag; all other items loose.

Strictly Prohibited:

  • No Glass (Use local Binnie recycling sites like Smith's Marketplace parking lot)
  • No plastic bags, grocery wraps, or film
Lost, Missing, or Broken Trash Cans

Washington County Solid Waste will repair or replace damaged or defective cans.

Submit a request online or call (435) 673-2813 ext. 2. Repairs are done on Thursdays. Leave the can out on the day after normal service. Writing your Unit Number on new cans with a permanent marker is recommended.

Large Item Disposal

Call Republic Services at (435) 628-2821 to schedule a pick-up date.

Provide your address (1921 W 540 N, 84770), unit number, and phone number. Place items out in front of your home 5 feet from your garbage can. Most large items are free; appliances containing refrigerant carry a $55 fee.

Hazardous Waste Disposal

The landfill accepts hazardous waste only during Household Hazardous Waste Days (held twice per year).

Accepted Hazardous Items: Aerosol cans, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, fuels, herbicides, medical waste, paint, pesticides, propane, and solvents.
