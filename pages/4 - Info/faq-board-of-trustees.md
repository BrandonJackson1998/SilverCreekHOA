### Can Homeowners attend Board meetings? 

**Absolutely!**

All owners are welcome for the regular session and may request to address the Board. Sessions are also made publicly available via Zoom for those unable to attend in person. 

* The agenda for the meeting will be posted on the Home Page prior to the meeting. 
* The current meeting's Zoom link is posted on the HOA website Home Page. 
* Zoom session info is also included in the agenda sent via email to each Homeowner. 

*Note: Board Meetings are held to conduct the official business of the HOA and are not interactive discussions. Attendees may address the Board during the Comments phase of the meeting and are limited to two minutes.*

### Vacancies on the Board 

Sometimes a Board member is unable to fulfill their entire term, leaving a vacancy on the Board.

To ensure continuity, distribute workload responsibilities, and maintain a quorum for meetings and decisions, the Board may make a temporary appointment to fill the position until the next regular Annual Meeting. 

Filling such positions is at the discretion of the Board and is not mandatory (e.g., if there is only a short time until the next Annual Meeting, the Board may defer the appointment to the next election). 

* The appointment will be made by consensus of existing Board Members. 
* The term is limited to the remaining time until the next Annual Meeting election. 
* The appointed position will be included with the regular vacancies during the next election cycle.
