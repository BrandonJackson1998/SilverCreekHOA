# Silver Creek Townhomes — St. George, Utah

**Address:** 1732 West 540 North, St. George, UT 84770  

Located between Westridge Drive and Dixie Drive in St. George, Utah, Silver Creek features no through streets, offering a quiet and safe low-traffic neighborhood.

---

## 🏡 HOA & Utilities Overview

Silver Creek provides low-maintenance, turn-key townhome living with managed amenities and community services.

* **HOA Dues:** $250/month via the PayHOA Portal.
* **Dues Cover:** Water, lawn and grounds maintenance, building exterior insurance, and access to all recreational facilities.
* **City Utilities:** Electricity, trash, and sewer are managed directly through the **City of St. George**.
* **Natural Gas:** **Enbridge Gas** (800-323-5517).
* **High-Speed Internet:** Choice between **Quantum Fiber** (Fiber-to-the-Premises) and **TDS Broadband**.

---

## 🏊 Community Amenities & Social Activities

### Resident Amenities
* **Pools & Spa:** Heated indoor pool, outdoor pool with waterfall, and hot tub.
* **Recreation:** Exercise room, library, and lighted basketball court.
* **Clubhouse:** Full clubhouse facility with kitchen for community gatherings.

### Active Social Calendar
* **Monthly Community Dinners:** Regular HOA social dinners.
* **Social Clubs:** Monthly Bunco games and resident gatherings.
* **Fitness:** Weekday water aerobics sessions in the pool.

*See the Newsletters and Calendars pages for upcoming event schedules.*

---

## 🚶 Walkability, Transit & Mobility

Silver Creek is a walkable, bikeable, and scooter-friendly community with convenient connection points:

* **Public Transit:** SunTran bus stops nearby, including **Route 6 (#133)** and **Route 4 (#87)** connecting to the Sunset Corner Transit Hub.
* **Trails:** Located ~0.5 miles from the **Halfway Wash Trailhead**, providing direct access to St. George's extensive paved trail system.
* **Micromobility:** Municipal Spin electric scooters operate throughout the immediate area.

---

## 🛒 Nearby Conveniences & Services

Silver Creek is within 2 miles of virtually every daily essential:

* **Groceries:** Albertson's (0.6 mi), Lin's Fresh Market (0.8 mi), Dixie Meats (1.2 mi), Digby's Market (2.3 mi), and Smith's Marketplace (2.6 mi).
* **Medical & Emergency:** Intermountain Health Sunset InstaCare (1.0 mi) and Fire Station 5 (0.6 mi).
* **Public Schools:** Sunset Elementary (0.5 mi), Snow Canyon Middle (1.7 mi), and Snow Canyon High School (1.7 mi).
* **Recreation & Shopping:** Minutes from local golf courses, the St. George Aquatic Center, Home Depot, and over 40 restaurants.

---

## 🔑 Information for Realtors & Prospective Buyers

* **Mailbox Keys:** New residents must present proof of residency at the **Main St Post Office** (180 N Main St) to receive mailbox keys.
* **HOA Documents:** Reversal disclosures, CCRs, and meeting minutes are accessible through the online HOA portal.
