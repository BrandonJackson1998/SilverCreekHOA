## Can Homeowners attend Board meetings? 

Absolutely!

All owners are welcome for the regular session and may request to address the Board. Sessions are also publicly made available as a Zoom meeting for those not able to attend in person. 

- The agenda for the meeting will be posted on the Home Page prior to the meeting. 
- The current meeting's Zoom link is posted on the HOA website Home Page. 
- Zoom session info is also posted in the agenda sent via email to each Homeowner. 

Board Meetings are to conduct the business of the HOA and are not interactive discussions with homeowners. Attendees may address the Board only during the Comments phase of the meeting and are limited to two minutes.

## Vacancies on the Board 

Sometimes a Board member is not able to fulfill their entire term and leaves a vacancy on the Board.

To assure continuity, the Board's ability to distribute responsibilities and workload, and to be able to establish a quorum for meetings and decisions, the Board may make a temporary appointment to fill the position until the next regular Annual Meeting. 

Filling such positions is at the discretion of the Board and is not mandatory. For example, if there is only a short time until the next Annual Meeting, the Board may defer the appointment and wait for the next election. 

- The appointment will be made by consensus of the existing Board Members. 
- The term is limited to the time remaining until the next Annual Meeting election. 
- The appointed position will be included with the three regular vacancies during the next Annual Meeting election.
